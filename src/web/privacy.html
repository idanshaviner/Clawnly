Privacy

Plain-language, alpha-stage version -- last updated September 2026.

Clawnly is an early, small-scale pilot. This page explains in plain language what we collect and why. It is written in good faith but is not a substitute for professional legal advice, and will be replaced with a proper policy before any wider launch.

What we collect

  • Your email address, used to sign you in (Google or an emailed sign-in link).
  • Whatever you tell your Claw during onboarding -- your own words, used to build a profile (things like your personality/energy, interests, availability, the group size you're after, and what you're hoping to get out of this). Nothing here is invented or imported from anywhere else; the conversation is the only source.
  • Whether you accept or decline a proposed match, and any feedback you give after a meetup.

How it's used

An AI ("Master Claw") reads profiles across your neighborhood to try to form small, genuinely compatible groups for a real-world meetup, and explains its reasoning. It is not reviewed by a human before it reaches you, unless something goes wrong and we need to look into it.

Who else sees it

  • Anthropic (Claude) processes your onboarding conversation and profile to build matches -- this is the core of how the product works.
  • Google or Resend, only as needed to sign you in, depending on which login method you use.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Your choices

You can stop at any time. Self-service account/data deletion is planned but not live yet -- until then, contact us and we'll handle it manually.
